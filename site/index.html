Search your vault inside Figma

Every note in your Obsidian vault, one search away from the canvas. Nothing leaves your machine.

Type once Find everything

Title, path, tags and body. Results in under a millisecond, with the matching notes lit up in the vault.

Drop a note on the canvas

A note becomes a Spec. A real Figma frame, so it moves, resizes and takes comments like everything else.

Linked Both ways

A thread ties the Spec to its note. Status sits right on the frame.

Edit in Obsidian Pull in Figma

Change the note on disk and Figma notices while the file is still open. Pull brings the line in.

Change the design Send it back

Size, fill, label and a deep link travel back into the note's Design section.
