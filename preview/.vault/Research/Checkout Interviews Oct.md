# Checkout Interviews Oct

Eight sessions, 30 min each, existing customers who abandoned a cart in the last 30 days.

## Themes
1. **Surprise costs** — shipping shown too late (6/8)
2. **Account wall** — forced sign-up kills momentum (5/8)
3. **Payment trust** — want Apple Pay, distrust typing cards on mobile (4/8)

> "I got to the end and it asked me to make an account. I just closed it." — P3

Feeds directly into [[Checkout Redesign]].
